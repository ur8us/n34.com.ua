# About This Archive

This article explains the purpose of the experimental archive and how its content should be developed over time.

## Purpose

The N34 Knowledge Base is being prepared as an experimental historical and technical archive. Its purpose is to organize knowledge about RigExpert-related products, people, technologies, milestones, and source materials in a form that can be reviewed, improved, and maintained over time.

The archive is not intended to replace source materials. It should help readers find, compare, and understand those materials through clear articles and source notes.

## What the Archive Will Contain

The archive is expected to include:

- product notes linked to the [Products](../products/index.md) section;
- historical milestones linked to the [Company History](../history/index.md) section;
- contributor and role notes linked to the [People](../people/index.md) section;
- technical notes linked to the [Technologies](../technologies/index.md) section;
- source records linked to [Sources and Archives](../sources/index.md);
- editorial instructions such as the [Editorial Guide](./editorial-guide.md).

Content may begin as a draft and improve over time. A draft can capture structure, questions, source leads, and incomplete notes before it becomes ready for review.

## Editorial Principles

Articles should be written in clear English, use descriptive headings, and separate verified facts from open questions. They should avoid unsupported claims and should not invent dates, people, product details, or historical events.

Human editors remain responsible for reviewing and approving published content. Every article should make its status clear so readers know whether it is a draft, under review, or approved for publication.

## Use of Artificial Intelligence

AI tools may assist with transcription, organization, translation, summarization, and editing. They may help turn raw notes into structured drafts or suggest clearer wording.

AI-generated text must not be treated as verified by default. A human reviewer must check factual claims, source references, names, dates, and publication readiness before content is approved.

## Sources and Verification

Factual claims should be supported by source notes where possible. Sources may include public pages, archived documents, photographs, interviews, manuals, release materials, or other records.

Source notes should be stored or referenced through [Sources and Archives](../sources/index.md). New source records can use the reusable template at `templates/source-note-template.md`.

## Current Status

This archive is in its first usable prototype stage. The structure is available, deployment is automated, and the editorial workflow is starting, but the archive does not yet contain a complete or official history.

The next editorial work is to collect source materials, define article owners, and begin drafting source-backed articles.
