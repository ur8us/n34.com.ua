# Editorial Guidelines

Use these draft rules when adding future wiki content.

## Writing Rules

- Write short pages with clear titles.
- Prefer dated facts over broad claims.
- Link to sources where possible.
- Separate verified history from draft notes.

## Review Rules

- Review people pages before publishing.
- Review product claims with the relevant owner.
- Keep internal-only content out of the public static site.
